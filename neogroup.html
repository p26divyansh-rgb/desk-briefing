NeoGroup — employer context

Best available match — confirm against the actual job description before the interview.

"NeoGroup" doesn't map cleanly to one well-known neobank. The closest, most plausible match is Neo Group / Neo Asset Management — an Indian alternative-investments platform (private credit, mid-market PE, real assets, and public-markets/credit trading), founded 2021 by Nitin Jain (former CEO, Edelweiss Wealth Management; ex-Goldman Sachs and Kotak Institutional Equities; IIT Kanpur / IIM Ahmedabad) and Hemant Daga. Runs roughly $3bn+ in assets under advisory, with a dedicated commodities/markets desk built by a former head of Edelweiss's commodities business — historically the largest such desk in the country.

What this implies: if this is the right firm, a "markets/trader" seat there is closer to a credit-and-macro trading desk inside an alternatives shop than a bank treasury desk — they likely want real, opinionated views on rates, currencies and commodities that inform positioning around the credit and real-asset books, not just textbook treasury/ALM answers. That supports leading with conviction — trade idea plus hedge — rather than hedged, textbook responses.

Concretely: this is the argument for opening with the power-sector trade or the metals trade (§3.9 / §3.10) rather than the cheat-sheet version of any answer — a credit-and-macro desk wants to hear the position and the hedge, not just the macro read.
